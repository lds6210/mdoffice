# Escalations — decisions needing the CEO

The Chief drops items here when CEO input is required to proceed. The CEO answers inline by editing the file. Resolved items get archived to `90_archive/escalations/<date>.md`.

Format:

```
## <timestamp> — <one-line summary of the decision>

Context: <2-3 lines, no more>
Options:
  A) <option>
  B) <option>
Chief's recommendation: <one of A/B/etc + one-line rationale>

CEO decision: (leave blank until decided)
```
